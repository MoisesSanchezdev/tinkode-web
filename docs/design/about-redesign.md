# TinKode About Redesign

## 1. Scope
Phase 6 redesigns only `/nosotros`.

## 2. Purpose
The page explains why TinKode exists, who forms the founder team and how their engineering profiles complement each other.

## 3. Editorial Rule
The page avoids fake history, fake photos, invented roles, offices, employees, awards, clients, metrics or corporate hierarchy.

## 4. Page Structure
The experience is limited to seven blocks: hero, origin, founder team, convergence, principles, trust and vision with CTA.

## 5. Hero
The hero uses the message: engineering, software and purpose. Its visual shows electronic engineering and systems engineering converging into TinKode.

## 6. Origin
The origin section is built around the question of designing technology around the problem instead of the reverse.

## 7. Founder Team
Moisés Sánchez Naranjo appears as Fundador. Valeria López Chávez appears as Cofundadora.

## 8. Education
Both profiles reference Universidad Tecnológica de Pereira and their current engineering studies.

## 9. Founder Visuals
Founder cards use initials and geometric technical patterns instead of portraits, stock photos or generated faces.

## 10. Moisés Focus
Moisés is associated with electronic engineering, automation, integration, software development and system design.

## 11. Valeria Focus
Valeria is associated with systems engineering, software development, architecture, organization and digital experiences.

## 12. Complementarity
The convergence block shows two contribution lanes meeting in TinKode, not a hierarchy or competition.

## 13. Principles
Five principles were selected: technology at the service of the business, purposeful engineering, adaptable solutions, quality over quantity and continuous learning.

## 14. Trust
The trust section answers credibility through working style: direct participation, customized solutions, clear communication, documentation, validation and continuous improvement.

## 15. Vision
The vision stays practical: useful, scalable solutions for businesses and organizations through engineering, software and automation.

## 16. CTA
The final CTA invites visitors to talk, review projects or share a process they want to improve.

## 17. Visual Identity
The design uses matte black, white, dark grays, TinKode neon green and TinKode electric blue.

## 18. Avoided Colors
The implementation avoids purple, pink, orange, yellow, red, rainbow treatments and intense cyan as dominant visual elements.

## 19. Components
The page is composed with Astro section components under `src/components/sections` and existing UI primitives.

## 20. Validation Target
The page must pass Astro check, production build, HTTP checks, content-role search and desktop/tablet/mobile visual review before commit.
